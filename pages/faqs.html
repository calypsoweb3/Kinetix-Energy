Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know?

How do I know if solar is right for my property?

It depends on factors such as your energy use, roof condition, orientation, shade, and local requirements. An assessment can help you understand which options are worth considering.

What happens during an energy assessment?

We start by learning about your goals and energy needs, then review relevant property details. The assessment helps inform a practical discussion of possible next steps.

Can solar work for a business?

Many businesses explore solar, but suitability depends on the site, operating patterns, energy demand, and project requirements. We can help you identify the questions to investigate.

How long does a solar project take?

Timing varies with the property, system design, permitting, equipment availability, and installation requirements. We can outline likely stages once we understand your project.

Can I add solar to an existing system?

Possibly. Existing equipment, system configuration, available roof or site space, and electrical requirements all need to be reviewed before planning an expansion.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Maintenance depends on the equipment and site conditions. Follow the guidance provided with your system, monitor for unusual changes, and arrange qualified help if something needs attention.

What should I prepare before contacting Kinetix?

A general idea of your property type, energy goals, and any questions is a helpful start. You do not need to have every project detail figured out before reaching out.
